Emotional Language Profiles of MLLMs

Analysis of empathic summary outputs using LIWC-22 linguistic categories. Higher Total Emotion indicates richer emotional vocabulary; higher Tone indicates warmer language.

Key insight: Models adopt fundamentally different empathic strategies. Gemini uses a clinical tone with high emotional acknowledgment. Qwen3-Omni and UniMoE-2.0 are warmer and more supportive. Smaller models (MiniCPM, VideoLLaMA2) show limited emotional modulation — they default to factual reporting.
